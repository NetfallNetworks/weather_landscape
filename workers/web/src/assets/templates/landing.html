the decoder ring

Every pixel earns its place

There are no labels in the landscape, only shapes. Here is what they mean — and the reading guide goes deeper.

Left edge — "you are here"
The house
Marks the present moment, at the leftmost pixel of the canvas. Smoke from the chimney tells you the atmospheric pressure.
Higher ground, warmer
The terrain
Higher ground, warmer air. The lower brown line is the actual temperature curve, drawn over the next 24 hours.
Forest — wind
The trees
Taller trees mean stronger wind. Their species — pine, palm, leafy, wind-bent — encodes the direction it's blowing from.
Sky cover
The clouds
More clouds, more cover. They appear above the part of the day they'll show up in.
Day markers
Sun & moon
The sun rises from where the day starts, the moon falls where it ends. The hours of daylight are visible at a glance.
Time stamps
The flowers
A blue bloom for midnight. A yellow one for noon. Anchors so you can locate any hour in the day timeline.
